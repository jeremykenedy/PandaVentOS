Hot warning

The factory firmware calls 50 °C hot and cannot be told otherwise. That suits PLA and not ABS, where the bed sits at a hundred all print.

Your own animation

Pick any image. Each row of it becomes one frame across the strip, and the Animation effect plays it. It is held in memory, so it is gone after a restart and has to be sent again. That is deliberate: keeping it would mean repartitioning the flash and losing the ability to go back to the factory firmware.
